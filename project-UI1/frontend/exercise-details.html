Push-Up

A bodyweight exercise that develops chest, shoulder, triceps, and core strength.

Exercise Overview

The push-up is a fundamental upper-body exercise. It uses your body weight to strengthen the chest, shoulders, triceps, and core without requiring gym equipment.

Muscle Group

Chest

Equipment

None

Difficulty

Beginner

Targeted Muscles

Chest Triceps Shoulders Core

How to Perform

  1. 1

    Starting Position

    Place your hands slightly wider than shoulder-width apart. Extend your legs and keep your body straight.

  2. 2

    Lower Your Body

    Bend your elbows and lower your chest toward the floor while keeping your core engaged.

  3. 3

    Push Back Up

    Push through your hands until your arms return to the starting position without locking your elbows.

  4. 4

    Repeat With Control

    Maintain a steady pace and breathe throughout each repetition.

Safety Tips

  • ✓ Keep your back and hips aligned.
  • ✓ Move slowly and maintain control.
  • ✓ Breathe naturally during each repetition.
  • ✓ Stop if you feel sharp or unusual pain.

Common Mistakes

  • • Allowing the hips to sag.
  • • Moving too quickly.
  • • Flaring the elbows excessively.
  • • Not maintaining a controlled movement.
