Push-Up

The push-up is a bodyweight exercise that strengthens the chest, shoulders, triceps, and core.

Exercise Information

  • Target muscles: Chest, shoulders, and triceps
  • Equipment: None
  • Difficulty: Beginner
  • Suggested sets: 3
  • Suggested repetitions: 10
Add to Routine

How to Perform

  1. Start position: Place your hands slightly wider than shoulder-width apart. Keep your body straight from head to heels.
  2. Lower your body: Bend your elbows and lower your chest toward the floor while keeping your core engaged.
  3. Push up: Press through your hands to return to the starting position.

Safety Tips

  • Keep your back and hips aligned.
  • Move slowly and maintain control.
  • Breathe steadily throughout the exercise.
  • Start with an easier variation if necessary.

Common Mistakes

  • Allowing the hips to sag.
  • Moving too quickly.
  • Flaring the elbows excessively.
  • Failing to use the full comfortable range of motion.
